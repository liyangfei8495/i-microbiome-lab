个人简介Bio

郑仕伟长期在德国从事植物抗逆与表观遗传研究，2026 年于德国完成博士学位（哈勒大学 & 莱布尼茨植物遗传与作物研究所，并先后于马克斯·普朗克分子植物生理研究所、波茨坦大学担任客座博士生）。 他的研究聚焦“植物如何在高温、低温等逆境中守住生育能力”：博士期间揭示热胁迫通过重编程 DNA 甲基化与染色质开放性破坏拟南芥雄配子育性的新机制，并挖掘关键调控基因；硕士期间阐明水稻细胞周期蛋白 OsCYCBL1 赋予低温耐受性的分子路径，为抗逆育种提供候选基因。 他具备扎实的分子生物学与高通量组学功底（ChIP-seq / ATAC-seq / 全基因组甲基化文库构建），并熟练运用 Linux 与 R 语言独立完成多组学整合分析。Dr. Zheng Shiwei is a young researcher who has spent years in Germany studying plant stress resilience and epigenetics, completing his doctorate in 2026 (University of Halle & Leibniz Institute of Plant Genetics and Crop Plant Research, with visiting stints at the Max Planck Institute of Molecular Plant Physiology and the University of Potsdam). His work asks how plants protect their fertility under heat and cold stress: during his PhD he revealed a novel epigenetic mechanism by which heat stress disrupts Arabidopsis male fertility through reprogramming of DNA methylation and chromatin accessibility, and identified key regulatory genes; his master's work elucidated how the rice cyclin gene OsCYCBL1 confers cold tolerance, providing candidate genes for stress-resilient breeding. He combines solid molecular biology with high-throughput omics (ChIP-seq / ATAC-seq / whole-genome methylation library preparation) and independently performs multi-omics integration using Linux and R.
