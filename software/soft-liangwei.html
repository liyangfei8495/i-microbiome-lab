软件简介About

量微（QuantScope）由付诸教育咨询工作室与智能微生态与生物制造实验室联合开发维护。面向照片与显微图像，提供从标定、测量到分析的完整定量流程：先用已知尺度（如标尺、培养皿直径、血球计数板格距）标定比例尺，即可对任意结构测量直线或曲线长度；面积与形态参数可得到选区的大小、周长、圆度、长宽比等量化指标；阈值分割与颗粒分析能自动识别并统计图像中的颗粒、菌落或细胞数量与平均尺寸；灰度直方图则用于观察亮度分布与对比度。所有测量结果可在表格中逐条编辑、增删与备注，并一键导出 CSV 供 Excel / R / Python 进一步处理。软件全程在本地运行，图像数据不出本机，适合教学演示、科研计量与日常图像分析。QuantScope is jointly developed and maintained by Fuzhu Education Consulting Studio and the Laboratory of Intelligent Microbiome and Biomanufacturing. Built for photographs and microscopy images, it offers a complete quantitative pipeline from calibration to analysis: calibrate the scale bar using a known reference (a ruler, petri-dish diameter, or hemocytometer grid spacing), then measure straight or curved lengths of any structure; area and shape parameters report size, perimeter, circularity and aspect ratio of a selection; threshold segmentation and particle analysis automatically detect and count particles, colonies or cells with their average size; a grayscale histogram shows brightness distribution and contrast. Every measurement can be edited, added, removed and annotated row by row in a table, then exported to CSV for Excel / R / Python. Everything runs locally — image data never leaves the machine, making it suitable for teaching demonstrations, research metrology and everyday image analysis.
