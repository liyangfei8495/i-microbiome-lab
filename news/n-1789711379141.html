【产学研合作】i-Microbiome Lab 赴晟氏绿色自然（四川）生物有限公司调研并开展团建活动[Industry-university-research cooperation] i-Microbiome Lab went to Sheng's Green Nature (Sichuan) Biotechnology Co., Ltd. to investigate and carry out group building activities

2026.09.16

2026年9月16日，西南科技大学智能微生态与生物制造实验室（i-Microbiome Lab）全体成员赴绵阳市北川羌族自治县，对晟氏绿色自然（四川）生物有限公司进行交流访问。本次行程旨在了解生物科技企业的实际发展状况与产业需求，探索校企协同创新与产学研合作的可行路径。

On September 16, 2026, all members of the Intelligent Microbiome and Biological Manufacturing Laboratory (i-Microbiome Lab) of Southwest University of Science and Technology went to Beichuan Qiang Autonomous County, Mianyang City for an exchange visit to Sheng's Green Nature (Sichuan) Biology Co., Ltd. The purpose of this trip is to understand the actual development status and industrial needs of biotechnology enterprises and explore feasible pathways for collaborative innovation and industry-university-research cooperation between schools and enterprises.

晟氏绿色自然（四川）生物有限公司（原四川晟氏生物医药有限公司，2025年底完成更名）成立于2021年1月，系晟氏健康科技集团核心成员企业，主要从事功能营养食品、食品添加剂、特色饮料及天然原料类产品的技术开发、生产制造与经营销售。公司现已打通从氨基酸原料、抗衰老因子到成品制剂与保健口服液的全产业链环节，并具备为客户提供 OEM/ODM 定制服务的能力。在生产条件方面，公司建有按 GMP 标准设计的生产车间，配备植物预处理、超临界提取与生物发酵工程等设备，并建立了较为完善的质量管理体系与检验流程。

Sheng's Green Nature (Sichuan) Biotechnology Co., Ltd. (formerly Sichuan Sheng's Biomedical Co., Ltd., renamed at the end of 2025) was established in January 2021 as a core member of Sheng's Health Technology Group. It is mainly engaged in the technical development, manufacturing and sales of functional nutritious foods, food additives, specialty beverages and natural raw material products. The company has now opened up the whole industry chain from amino acid raw materials and anti-aging factors to finished products and health oral solutions, and has the ability to provide customers with OEM/ODM customized services. In terms of production conditions, the company has established a production workshop designed according to GMP standards, equipped with plant pretreatment, supercritical extraction, biological fermentation engineering and other equipment, and has established a relatively complete quality management system and inspection process.

访问期间，实验室成员在公司接待人员的引导下，依次参观了生产工厂与科研中心。参观涵盖从原料预处理、萃取纯化到灌装包装的主要生产环节，双方就相关工艺原理、设备选型与研发条件进行了现场交流。科研中心部分则集中展示了企业的技术创新平台与在研方向。

During the visit, under the guidance of the company's reception staff, the laboratory members visited the production plant and the scientific research center in turn. The visit covered the main production links from raw material pretreatment, extraction and purification to filling and packaging, and the two sides had on-site exchanges on relevant process principles, equipment selection and R&D conditions. The research center section focuses on the technology innovation platform and research direction of the enterprise.

座谈环节，晟氏生物方面介绍了企业在天然产物高值化利用、功能成分稳态化、工艺降本与标准化等方面的技术需求。梁元可教授及随行团队成员结合 i-Microbiome Lab 在智能微生态与生物制造方向的研究积累，就微生物组资源开发、功能菌株挖掘及生物制造工艺等可能的合作切入点进行了交流，并表达了在产学研协同、技术攻关与人才培养等方面开展合作的意愿。双方一致认为，高校的基础研究能力与企业的工程化、市场化优势具有较强互补性，具备进一步对接的空间。

During the discussion session, Sheng Biotechnology introduced the technical needs of the enterprise in terms of the high-value utilization of natural products, the stabilization of functional ingredients, process cost reduction and standardization. Professor Liang Yuanke and accompanying team members combined with i-Microbiome Lab's research and accumulation in the direction of intelligent microbiology and biomanufacturing, exchanged possible entry points for cooperation in microbiome resource development, functional strain mining and biomanufacturing processes, and expressed their willingness to cooperate in industry-university-research collaboration, technical research and talent training. The two sides agreed that the basic research capabilities of colleges and universities are highly complementary to the engineering and market-oriented advantages of enterprises, and there is room for further docking.

访问当日，实验室组织了组建以来的首次全体成员团建活动，赴石椅羌寨开展踏青。此次活动为师生在科研工作之余提供了交流平台，有助于缓解科研压力、增进师生情谊、增强团队凝聚力。

On the day of the visit, the laboratory organized the first group building activity for all members since its establishment, and went to Shichi Qiangzhai to carry out Taqing. This activity provides an exchange platform for teachers and students to work outside of scientific research, helping to alleviate the pressure of scientific research, enhance the friendship between teachers and students, and enhance team cohesion.

此次北川之行兼具业务交流与团队建设的双重意义。通过走访晟氏生物，实验室师生增进了对生物科技企业发展阶段与现实需求的了解，为后续校企协同创新与产学研合作奠定了基础；同期开展的团建活动则进一步增强了团队凝聚力与归属感。双方约定保持沟通，推动具体合作方向的进一步落实。

This trip to Beichuan has the dual significance of business exchanges and team building. Through visiting Sheng Biotechnology, laboratory teachers and students have improved their understanding of the development stage and actual needs of biotechnology enterprises, laying the foundation for follow-up school-enterprise collaborative innovation and industry-university-research cooperation; the team building activities carried out during the same period have further enhanced team cohesion and sense of belonging. The two sides agreed to maintain communication and promote the further implementation of specific cooperation directions.
