【学术交流】山东大学李磊博士后到访 i‑Microbiome Lab 开展前沿学术报告[Academic Exchange] Postdoctoral visit by Li Lei of Shandong University to i ‑ Microbiome Lab to carry out cutting-edge academic reports

2026.09.21

2026 年 9 月 21 日晚 20:00，应梁元可教授i‑Microbiome Lab课题组邀请，山东大学李磊博士后于东 9B2‑45 会议室作专题学术报告，实验室及院内多名师生到场参会。

At 20:00 on the evening of September 21, 2026, at the invitation of Professor Liang Yuanke's * * i ‑ Microbiome Lab * * research group, Dr. Li Lei of Shandong University gave a special academic report in East 9B2 ‑ 45 Conference Room, and a number of teachers and students from the laboratory and the hospital attended the meeting.

报告中，李磊博士后围绕大豆空间转录组的前沿探索工作展开汇报，系统阐述该技术在大豆研究中的实验方案、原位基因表达解析策略以及组学大数据挖掘思路，深入剖析当前研究存在的技术瓶颈与可行优化路径。依托自身科研实践经验，他进一步分享人工智能在生命科学研究中的高阶应用方案，结合组学数据处理、科研假说构建等实际场景介绍落地方法，为参会师生带来跨学科研究的新思路。

In the report, Dr. Li Lei reported on the cutting-edge exploration work of the soybean spatial transcriptome, systematically expounded the experimental scheme, in situ gene expression analysis strategy and big data mining ideas of the technology in soybean research, and deeply analyzed the technical bottlenecks and feasible optimization pathways of the current research. Relying on his own practical experience in scientific research, he further shared the high-level application program of artificial intelligence in life science research, combined with the introduction of landing methods in practical scenarios such as oncology data processing and scientific research hypothesis construction, and brought new ideas for interdisciplinary research to the participating teachers and students.

在交流研讨环节，与会师生就空间转录组样本前处理、测序数据分析流程、AI 科研工具选型与参数调试等问题同报告人展开充分讨论，现场研讨氛围热烈。

During the exchange and discussion session, the teachers and students had a full discussion with the presenter on issues such as spatial transcriptome sample pretreatment, sequencing data analysis process, AI scientific research tool selection and parameter debugging, and the on-site discussion was enthusiastic.

本次学术交流打通了校际科研沟通渠道，将前沿空间组学技术与 AI 辅助科研理念引入课题组，拓宽了团队成员的研究视野，为后续微生物组、多组学交叉方向的课题布局提供了新的切入点，对推动实验室科研方法创新、培育交叉型科研思维具有积极长远意义。

This academic exchange has opened up intercollegiate scientific research communication channels, introduced cutting-edge space genomics technology and AI-assisted scientific research concepts into the research group, broadened the research horizons of team members, provided a new entry point for the follow-up microbiome and multi-omics cross-directional topic layout, and has positive long-term significance for promoting laboratory scientific research method innovation and fostering cross-disciplinary scientific research thinking.
