【科研进展】i‑Microbiome Lab 发表首篇 SCI 综述：梳理蚯蚓堆肥促氮效应的基质—微生物研究[Scientific progress] i ‑ Microbiome Lab published the first SCI review: Combing the matrix of nitrogen-promoting effects of earthworm compost — microbial research

2026.09.28
近日，i‑Microbiome Lab（智能微生态与生物制造实验室）一篇综述论文在线发表于国际期刊 Frontiers in Plant Science（Sec. Plant Symbiotic Interactions）。这是该实验室组建以来的首篇 SCI 综述。

Recently, a review paper from the i ‑ Microbiome Lab was published online in the international journal Frontiers in Plant Science (Sec. Plant Symbiotic Interactions). This is the first SCI review since the establishment of the laboratory.

论文题为 Deciphering the Nitrogen‑Promoting Effect of Vermicompost: From a Complex Organic Matrix to Synthetic Microbial Communities。2026 级生物学硕士研究生李杨飞（西南科技大学生命科学与农林学院、i‑Microbiome Lab）为第一作者，黄晶教授（西南科技大学生命科学与农林学院院长）参与本项研究，梁元可教授（西南科技大学生命科学与农林学院、i‑Microbiome Lab 实验室负责人）担任通讯作者。研究由西南科技大学高层次科研人员启动基金资助。

The paper is entitled Deciphering the Nitrogen ‑ Promoting Effect of Vermicompost: From a Complex Organic Matrix to Synthetic Microbial Communities. Yang Fei Li, Master of Biology 2026 (i ‑ Microbiome Lab, School of Life Sciences and Agroforestry, Southwestern University of Science and Technology) is the lead author, Professor Huang Jing (Dean, School of Life Sciences and Agroforestry, Southwestern University of Science and Technology) is involved in the study, and Professor Liang Yuanke (Head of i ‑ Microbiome Lab, School of Life Sciences and Agroforestry, Southwestern University of Science and Technology) is the corresponding author. The research is funded by the Southwest University of Science and Technology's high-level research staff start-up fund.

i‑Microbiome Lab 聚焦微生物组与生物制造交叉方向，围绕农业微生物资源挖掘、功能菌群设计与田间应用开展研究。本项工作是实验室组建初期对相关研究方向的一次文献整理。

i ‑ Microbiome Lab focuses on the intersection of the microbiome and biomanufacturing, and conducts research on agricultural microbial resource mining, functional flora design, and field applications. This work is a literature compilation of relevant research directions in the early stage of laboratory establishment.

一、研究背景与综述立项意义

I. Research Background and Summary Project Significance

我国农业生产中长期存在化学氮肥过量投入、作物氮素利用效率偏低的问题。田间观测显示，单施化学氮肥时约 30%–50% 的氮素被作物吸收，其余经淋溶、氨挥发、反硝化等途径损失，既造成资源浪费，也带来环境压力。

There are problems of excessive input of chemical nitrogen fertilizer and low nitrogen utilization efficiency of crops in agricultural production in China for a long time. Field observations show that about 30% –50% of nitrogen is absorbed by crops when chemical nitrogen fertilizer is applied alone, and the rest is lost through leaching, ammonia volatilization, denitrification, etc., resulting in waste of resources and environmental pressure.

蚯蚓堆肥由有机废弃物经蚯蚓与微生物协同转化形成，已有大量盆栽与田间试验观察到其在改良土壤、辅助作物氮素获取方面的作用。但既往研究大多只报告“施用后产量如何”，对于促氮作用究竟来自堆肥中的有机质，还是其中的微生物，以及二者如何配合，一直缺少集中的讨论。这篇综述即在广泛阅读已有文献的基础上，尝试把分散的观察组织到同一个视角下。

Earthworm compost is formed by the synergistic transformation of organic waste by earthworms and microorganisms. A large number of potted plants and field experiments have observed its role in improving soil and auxiliary crop nitrogen acquisition. However, most previous studies have only reported "what is the yield after application". There has been a lack of focused discussion on whether the nitrogen-promoting effect comes from the organic matter in the compost, the microorganisms in it, and how the two cooperate. This review is an attempt to organize scattered observations into the same perspective based on extensive reading of existing literature.

二、蚯蚓堆肥对作物氮素吸收的整体调控效应

II. Overall Regulatory Effect of Earthworm Compost on Crop Nitrogen Absorption

综述首先汇总已有文献中关于蚯蚓堆肥施用后作物与土壤变化的报道。施用蚯蚓堆肥可提升土壤总氮与有效氮库容，富集氮循环相关微生物，改变根际群落结构；在根构型与共生表型方面，已有报道观察到蚯蚓堆肥促进侧根与根毛增殖、增强豌豆根瘤与菌根真菌定殖、配施丛枝菌根（AM）真菌促进柠檬草生长等现象（原文表 1）。作者同时指出，此类结果多为表型记录，直接机制证据有限。

The review first summarizes the existing literature reports on crop and soil changes after the application of earthworm compost. The application of earthworm compost can enhance the total nitrogen and effective nitrogen storage capacity of the soil, enrich the microorganisms related to the nitrogen cycle, and change the rhizosphere community structure. In terms of root configuration and symbiotic phenotype, it has been reported that earthworm compost promotes the proliferation of lateral roots and root hairs, enhances the colonization of pea root nodules and mycorrhizal fungi, and combines the application of arbuscular mycorrhizal (AM) fungi to promote the growth of lemongrass (Table 1 of the original text). At the same time, the authors point out that most of these results are phenotypic records, and the direct mechanism evidence is limited.

Figure 1 shows how crops absorb nitrogen through Amt (ammonium transporter) and NRT (nitrate transporter) after compost-related microorganisms drive rhizospheric nitrogen mineralization, and complete intracellular assimilation through the GS ‑ GOGAT pathway.

（图 1 蚯蚓堆肥介导作物根系氮素获取的可能途径，包括微生物驱动根际氮矿化、AMT/NRT 转运蛋白吸收与 GS‑GOGAT 胞内同化。来源：Li et al., Front. Plant Sci., 2026，CC‑BY 4.0）

(Figure 1 Possible pathways for crop root nitrogen capture mediated by earthworm compost include microbial-driven rhizospheric nitrogen mineralization, Amt/NRT transporter uptake, and GS ‑ GOGAT intracellular assimilation. Source: Li et al., Front. Plant Sci., 2026, CC ‑ BY 4.0)

三、不同施肥模式的氮素分配特征与田间表现

III. Nitrogen distribution characteristics and field performance of different fertilization modes

综述汇总了已发表的施肥模式对比研究。不同作者在不同条件下的结果可以归纳为以下几点：

  1. 单施化学氮肥。多数报道显示氮素环境损失比例较高。

  2. 单施蚯蚓堆肥。多数报道显示供氮平缓、损失较低，但速效供氮能力有限。

  3. 化肥配施蚯蚓堆肥。已有报道显示，在部分条件下可以同时获得较平稳的供氮和较低的损失。

The review summarizes published comparative studies of fertilization patterns. The results of different authors under different conditions can be summarized as follows: single application of chemical nitrogen fertilizer. Most reports indicate a high proportion of nitrogen environmental losses. Apply earthworm compost alone. Most reports show that the nitrogen supply is moderate and the loss is low, but the quick-acting nitrogen supply capacity is limited. Fertilizer is combined with earthworm compost. It has been reported that a smoother nitrogen supply and lower losses can be obtained at the same time under some conditions.

图 2 对已有文献中三种模式的氮素分配作了示意性比较。

Figure 2 shows a schematic comparison of the three modes of nitrogen allocation in the existing literature.

（图 2 三种施肥模式下土壤氮素分配与环境损失对比（据已有文献归纳示意）。来源：Li et al., Front. Plant Sci., 2026，CC‑BY 4.0）

(Figure 2 Comparison of soil nitrogen allocation and environmental loss under the three fertilization modes (according to the existing literature). Source: Li et al., Front. Plant Sci., 2026, CC ‑ BY 4.0)

已有田间试验提供了具体证据。在减施矿质氮的条件下配施蚯蚓堆肥，玉米产量可维持全量化肥水平，植株总氮吸收量达到 112.6 kg/ha；在辣椒、豆类和谷物上也观察到类似的正向趋势，例如辣椒上相对农户习惯施肥，氮磷钾积累量提高 17%–58%。作者强调，这些结果受作物、土壤和管理条件影响，不能简单外推。

Field trials have provided concrete evidence. With the application of earthworm compost under the condition of mineral nitrogen reduction, the yield of maize can maintain the level of full chemical fertilizer, and the total nitrogen absorption of plants reaches 112.6 kg/ha. Similar positive trends have been observed in peppers, legumes, and grains. For example, the accumulation of nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium on peppers increased by 17% –58% compared with the customary fertilization of farmers. The authors emphasize that these results are influenced by crop, soil, and management conditions and cannot be simply extrapolated.

四、基质与微生物协同促氮机制解析

IV. Analysis of the synergistic nitrogen-promoting mechanism between matrix and microorganisms

在现象观察的基础上，综述尝试从“基质”和“微生物”两个维度理解上述效应。这是综述在归纳已有文献后提出的一个组织视角，并非新的实验结论。

On the basis of phenomenon observation, the review attempts to understand the above effects from the two dimensions of "matrix" and "microorganism". This is an organizational perspective proposed after reviewing the existing literature, and is not a new experimental conclusion.

（一）关于基质的作用。 综合已有报道，有机质基质在三方面被反复提及。

  1. 养分缓释。腐殖胶体结合有机氮，在土壤中缓慢矿化，避免一次性释放造成流失。

  2. 保氮固持。腐殖胶体带负电荷，通过阳离子交换吸附铵离子，减少氮随水淋失。

  3. 提供微生境。腐殖质为微生物提供碳源和栖息空间，被认为是微生物在堆肥和土壤中定居繁殖的重要条件。

(1) About the role of the matrix. Taken together, it has been reported that the organic matrix has been repeatedly mentioned in three aspects. Slow release of nutrients. Humus colloids combine with organic nitrogen to slowly mineralize in the soil and avoid loss due to one-time release. Nitrogen retention. Humus colloids are negatively charged, and ammonium ions are adsorbed by cation exchange to reduce nitrogen leaching with water. Provides microhabitats. Humus provides a carbon source and habitat for microorganisms, and is considered an important condition for microorganisms to settle and reproduce in compost and soil.

（二）关于微生物的来源。 关于堆肥中功能微生物从何而来，已有研究主要提出两条富集途径。

  1. 体内途径（蚯蚓肠道）。蚯蚓取食有机物料时，肠道对微生物进行选择性筛选和富集，耐逆、促生菌株随蚓粪排出。

  2. 体外途径（蚓触圈）。堆肥施入土壤后，蚯蚓活动形成的蚓触圈（drilosphere，即蚯蚓活动形成的特殊微环境）不同于周围原土，其中的黏附物质、养分梯度和 pH 条件被认为会激活土壤中原本休眠的土著微生物，包括与氮循环相关的类群。

(2) Sources of microorganisms. Regarding the origin of functional microorganisms in compost, two enrichment pathways have been proposed. In vivo pathway (earthworm intestine). When earthworms feed on organic materials, the intestines selectively screen and enrich microorganisms, and stress-resistant and growth-promoting strains are excreted with vermicompost. Extracorporeal pathways (worm antennae). After the compost is applied to the soil, the drilosphere (the special microenvironment formed by earthworm activity) formed by earthworm activity is different from the surrounding original soil, and the adhesion substances, nutrient gradients, and pH conditions are thought to activate the dormant indigenous microorganisms in the soil, including the groups related to the nitrogen cycle.

（三）关于微生物的作用。 综合已有研究，堆肥相关微生物对作物氮获取的影响可归为直接和间接两类。

  1. 直接作用。已有报道记录了微生物参与有机氮矿化、生物固氮、硝化等氮素形态转化过程，被认为直接改变根际有效氮库。

  2. 间接作用。部分文献提示，微生物可能通过分泌植物激素或信号分子，影响作物根系构型，并与氮转运相关基因（AMT、NRT 家族）的表达变化相关。作者同时指出，这一间接通路在蚯蚓堆肥体系中的直接实验证据仍有限，现有讨论多参照一般植物—微生物互作体系。

(3) Regarding the role of microorganisms. Combined with existing studies, the effects of compost-related microorganisms on crop nitrogen access can be classified into two categories: direct and indirect. Direct action. Microorganisms have been reported to participate in the transformation of nitrogen forms such as organic nitrogen mineralization, biological nitrogen fixation, and nitrification, which is considered to directly change the rhizosphere effective nitrogen pool. Indirect action. Some literature suggests that microorganisms may affect crop root configuration by secreting plant hormones or signaling molecules and are associated with changes in the expression of nitrogen transport-related genes (Amt, NRT family). The authors also pointed out that the direct experimental evidence of this indirect pathway in the earthworm composting system is still limited, and the existing discussions refer to the general plant-microbial interaction system.

Figure 3 shows the above two enrichment pathways, direct and indirect action pathways, and finally the process of sinking into the rhizospheric effective nitrogen reservoir.

（图 3 蚯蚓堆肥微生物介导氮转化过程示意图，包括蚯蚓肠道体内筛选、蚓触圈体外激活两条富集途径，以及直接氮转化与间接调控植物氮获取的通路。图件由 BioGDP.com 绘制。来源：Li et al., Front. Plant Sci., 2026，CC‑BY 4.0）

（四）两个维度之间的关系。 在归纳上述文献的基础上，作者提出，基质和微生物在时间上可能存在先后衔接。基质先行构建物质与环境条件，包括提供碳源、固定铵离子、营造微生境；微生物在此基础上参与氮素转化和对作物的信号影响。作者尝试用“基质启动、微生物放大”来概括这一关系。


(4) The relationship between the two dimensions. On the basis of summarizing the above literature, the authors suggest that there may be a temporal connection between the matrix and microorganisms. The substrate first builds material and environmental conditions, including providing a carbon source, fixing ammonium ions, and creating microhabitats; on this basis, microorganisms are involved in nitrogen conversion and signal influence on crops. The authors attempted to summarize this relationship with “matrix initiation, microbial amplification”.

五、应用价值与产品质控体系讨论

V. Discussion on application value and product quality control system

在文献归纳的基础上，综述进一步讨论了上述认识在产品研发和生产中的可能含义。

On the basis of the literature summary, the review further discusses the possible implications of the above knowledge in product development and production.

（一）合成微生物群落的思路。 已有研究普遍观察到，单一菌株施入大田后环境适应性弱、功能不稳定。综述据此讨论了从蚯蚓堆肥中筛选氮循环功能菌株，并按模块化设计与功能冗余原则组装合成微生物群落（SynCom）的思路，认为这可能为提升菌群田间稳定性提供一条路径。

(1) Ideas for synthetic microbial communities. It has been generally observed in previous studies that a single strain has weak environmental adaptability and unstable function after being applied to the field. Based on this review, we discussed the idea of screening functional strains of nitrogen cycle from earthworm compost and assembling synthetic microbial communities (SynCom) according to the principles of modular design and functional redundancy, which may provide a pathway to improve the stability of microbial communities in the field.

（二）产品质量控制参数。 综述梳理蚯蚓堆肥从原料预处理、受控发酵到成品检测全流程，在原文表 2 中归纳出涵盖化学、生物、物理三类的质控参考框架。作者指出，对比国内外现有标准，当前规范大多聚焦养分、重金属等理化安全指标，针对蚯蚓堆肥生物促氮能力的功能评价，尚未建立统一、可量化的指标体系，这是制约蚯蚓堆肥产品规范化应用的关键瓶颈。

(2) Product quality control parameters. Summarize the entire process of combing earthworm compost from raw material pretreatment, controlled fermentation to finished product testing, and summarize the quality control reference framework covering chemical, biological, and physical categories in Table 2 of the original text. The author points out that compared with existing standards at home and abroad, the current norms mostly focus on physical and chemical safety indicators such as nutrients and heavy metals. For the functional evaluation of the biological nitrogen-promoting capacity of earthworm composting, a unified and quantifiable index system has not yet been established, which is a key bottleneck restricting the standardized application of earthworm composting products.

（三）田间效果的综合观察。 原文表 3 汇总了多类作物盆栽与田间试验的已有报道。已有文献观察到，配施蚯蚓堆肥有助于改善作物产量、氮素利用效率与土壤健康状态，具备经济、环境与生态层面的综合效益。作者同时指出，效益幅度受土壤、作物和管理条件调节。

(3) Comprehensive observation of field effects. Table 3 of the original article summarizes the existing reports on potting and field trials of various crops. It has been observed in the literature that the application of earthworm compost helps to improve crop yield, nitrogen utilization efficiency and soil health, and has comprehensive economic, environmental and ecological benefits. At the same time, the authors point out that benefit margins are regulated by soil, crops, and management conditions.

六、领域研究短板与未来研究方向

VI. Field research shortcomings and future research directions

基于文献梳理，论文归纳出当前该领域尚存的若干关键科学短板。

  1. 定量问题。基质与微生物各自对促氮效应的贡献比例，目前缺少标准化的解析方法。

  2. 定殖问题。功能菌株施入田间后的原位定殖和种群演变规律，已有研究仍不充分。

  3. 标准问题。产品层面的生物促氮功能评价指标，目前缺失。

  4. 互作问题。蚯蚓堆肥与植物根际共生微生物之间的互作，直接实验证据仍有限。

这些问题也是 i‑Microbiome Lab 后续研究计划准备回应的方向。

Based on the literature, the paper summarizes some key scientific shortcomings in this field. Quantitative issues. The ratio of the contribution of the substrate to the microorganism to the nitrogen-promoting effect, and the lack of standardized analytical methods. Colonization problem. The in-situ colonization and population evolution of functional strains after application to the field are still insufficiently studied. Standard question. Evaluation indicator of biological nitrogen-promoting function at the product level, currently missing. Interaction problem. Direct experimental evidence of interaction between earthworm compost and symbiotic microorganisms in the rhizosphere of plants is still limited. These questions are also the direction in which i ‑ Microbiome Lab's follow-up study plan is prepared to respond.

结语

Conclusion

作为一篇叙述性综述，这篇文章本身不报告新的实验数据，而是在阅读和整理已有文献的基础上，尝试提供一个把“基质—微生物—作物”放在一起理解的角度。它的价值在于把过去分散的观察组织到同一框架下，并把现有研究的空白点明确指出来，供后续试验设计参考。后续 i‑Microbiome Lab 将围绕文中提到的若干未决问题开展工作。

As a narrative review, the article itself does not report new experimental data, but rather attempts to provide a perspective that puts “matrix-microorganism-crop” together on the basis of reading and collating existing literature. Its value lies in organizing the scattered observations of the past under the same framework, and clearly pointing out the gaps in the existing research for reference in the subsequent trial design. Subsequently, the i ‑ Microbiome Lab will work on a number of the outstanding issues mentioned in this article.
