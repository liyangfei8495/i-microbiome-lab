【博学论坛】梁元可教授邀请马普所博士生李正洲作客第48期博学讲坛，共话 AI 时代科研工作流[Academic Forum] Professor Liang Yuanke invited Ph.D. student Li Zhengzhou of the Mapu Institute as the guest of the 48th Academic Forum to talk about the workflow of scientific research in the AI era

2026.09.18

2026年9月18日19:00，西南科技大学第48期博学讲坛在东11楼B201/B203举行。本期讲坛由实验室梁元可教授邀请德国马克斯·普朗克研究所博士生李正洲联合报告，欢迎全校师生参加。现场座无虚席，气氛热烈。

At 19:00 on September 18, 2026, the 48th Southwest University of Science and Technology Lecture Forum was held at B201/B203 on the east 11th floor. Professor Liang Yuanke of the laboratory invited Li Zhengzhou, a doctoral student at the Max Planck Institute in Germany, to give a joint report at this forum. The whole university is welcome to participate. There were no empty seats at the scene, and the atmosphere was warm.

李正洲为马克斯·普朗克研究所博士二年级在读，硕士阶段主要从事玉米育种研究，博士阶段转向水稻生物信息学与多组学研究，目前主要通过基因组学、转录组学、代谢组学等多组学数据，结合 GWAS、群体遗传学和生物信息学分析，挖掘控制水稻品质形成的关键基因及其遗传机制，参与发表 Nature Plants 综述，并持续开展水稻品质相关遗传与多组学研究。他以《AI 时代的科研工作流与研究生成长经验分享》为题，结合自身从作物育种到多组学研究的科研转型经历，与现场同学分享了 AI 时代的科研工作流搭建与研究生成长体会。

Li Zhengzhou is studying for the second year of his PhD at the Max Planck Institute. At the master's stage, he is mainly engaged in maize breeding research. At the doctoral stage, he has turned to rice bioinformatics and multiomics research. At present, through genomics, transcriptomics, metabolomics and other multiomics data, combined with GWAS, population genetics and bioinformatics analysis, he has excavated the key genes that control the formation of rice quality and their genetic mechanisms, participated in the publication of the Nature Plants review, and continued to carry out research on rice quality-related genetics and multiomics. Taking the title of "Scientific Research Workflow and Postgraduate Growth Experience Sharing in the AI Era", he combined his own experience of scientific research transformation from crop breeding to multi-omics research, and shared with the field students the experience of scientific research workflow construction and postgraduate growth in the AI era.

随后，梁元可教授以《工具在变，判断力不变》为题作报告。梁元可博士、教授、博士生导师，任职于西南科技大学生命科学与农林学院，博士毕业于比利时根特大学，先后在瑞士日内瓦大学、德国马普所等顶尖机构从事博士后研究。长期聚焦植物应激响应与再生机制，利用 CRISPR 基因编辑与多组学技术，系统阐明了 ERF 转录因子驱动植物损伤后高效再生的分子通路，并鉴定出全新的紫外光 UV-B 光信号响应和保护代谢物。以第一作者在《Science Advances》等国际顶尖期刊发表多篇论文，其研究为作物抗逆育种和现代农业发展提供了重要的理论基础与全新的基因靶点。入选国家级青年人才项目，主持国家自然科学基金青年基金，担任多个国际期刊副编委。他在报告中与同学们分享了在科研工具快速迭代的时代，如何坚守科学判断力这一研究者的立身之本。

Subsequently, Professor Liang Yuanke made a presentation entitled "Tools are changing, judgement remains unchanged". Dr. Liang Yuanke, professor, doctoral supervisor, worked in the School of Life Sciences and Agriculture and Forestry of Southwestern University of Science and Technology, and graduated from the University of Ghent in Belgium. He has successively engaged in postdoctoral research at leading institutions such as the University of Geneva in Switzerland and the Mapu Institute in Germany. With a long-term focus on plant stress response and regeneration mechanisms, CRISPR gene editing and multi-omics technology have been used to systematically elucidate the molecular pathways of ERF transcription factors driving efficient regeneration after plant damage, and identify new UV-B light signal responses and protective metabolites. The first author has published several papers in leading international journals such as Science Advances, and his research has provided an important theoretical basis and new genetic targets for crop stress resistance breeding and modern agricultural development. Selected for the National Young Talent Program, presided over the National Natural Science Foundation Youth Fund, and served as a deputy editorial board member of several international journals. In the report, he shared with his classmates how to adhere to the foundation of scientific judgment in the era of rapid iteration of scientific research tools.

互动环节中，现场同学踊跃提问，两位报告人逐一细致解答。讲坛在热烈的掌声中圆满结束。

During the interactive session, the classmates on the scene were enthusiastic about asking questions, and the two presenters gave detailed answers one by one. The podium ended with loud applause.

本次讲坛内容贴近科研一线，兼具方法与实操，为同学们在新学期伊始审视自己的科研工作方式、善用新工具提升研究效率提供了切实参考。

The content of this forum is close to the front line of scientific research, and it has both methods and practices, which provides practical reference for students to review their own scientific research work methods at the beginning of the new semester and make good use of new tools to improve research efficiency.
