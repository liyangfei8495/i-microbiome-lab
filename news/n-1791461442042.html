【学术交流】广州大学张淼博士应邀为i‑Microbiome Lab作线上学术报告[Academic Exchange] Dr. Zhang Miao of Guangzhou University was invited to give an online academic report for i ‑ Microbiome Lab

2026.10.8
近日，为进一步拓宽科研视野、促进作物分子遗传与逆境生物学方向的学术交流，梁元可教授领衔的i‑Microbiome Lab通过腾讯会议线上平台，邀请广州大学张淼博士开展专题学术报告，课题组全体师生参会学习。

Recently, in order to further broaden the horizon of scientific research and promote academic exchanges in the direction of crop molecular genetics and adversity biology, Professor Liang Yuanke's i ‑ Microbiome Lab invited Dr. Zhang Miao of Guangzhou University to carry out special academic reports through the Tencent Conference online platform, and all teachers and students in the research group participated in the study.

张淼博士现为广州大学创新分子遗传与进化中心博士后研究员，2024年6月获哈尔滨师范大学遗传学博士学位，主要从事作物耐盐、耐重金属胁迫分子调控机制研究。目前以第一作者发表SCI论文3篇，授权国家发明专利1项，主持广州地区联合基金项目1项，熟练掌握作物遗传转化、CRISPR基因编辑、多组学分析及转录调控验证等关键技术体系。

Dr. Zhang Miao is currently a postdoctoral researcher at the Innovative Molecular Genetics and Evolution Center of Guangzhou University. In June 2024, he obtained a doctorate in genetics from Harbin Normal University. He is mainly engaged in the study of molecular regulation mechanisms of crop salt tolerance and heavy metal tolerance. At present, the first author has published 3 SCI papers, authorized 1 national invention patent, presided over 1 joint fund project in Guangzhou, and is proficient in key technology systems such as crop genetic transformation, CRISPR gene editing, multi-omics analysis, and transcriptional regulation verification.

报告中，张淼博士系统介绍了其近期代表性研究成果。在博士后研究阶段，团队聚焦大豆光周期转录因子E1，揭示其通过直接抑制GmCIPK25表达、负调控大豆耐盐性的分子机制，相关成果已投稿至《Journal of Genetics and Genomics》。

In the report, Dr. Zhang Miao systematically introduced his recent representative research results. In the postdoctoral research phase, the team focused on soybean photoperiod transcription factor E1 to reveal its molecular mechanism of direct inhibition of GmCIPK25 expression and negative regulation of soybean salt tolerance. The relevant results have been submitted to the Journal of Genetics and Genomics.

报告结束后，师生围绕作物抗逆基因挖掘、转录调控机制、多组学数据分析与基因编辑技术应用等问题展开深入交流，讨论氛围浓厚，有效促进了学科思路互通与技术经验共享。

After the report, teachers and students conducted in-depth exchanges on issues such as crop stress resistance gene mining, transcriptional regulation mechanisms, multi-omics data analysis, and gene editing technology applications. The atmosphere of discussion was strong, which effectively promoted the exchange of disciplinary ideas and the sharing of technical experience.

本次线上学术交流内容前沿、成果扎实，有效拓宽了课题组在作物逆境分子生物学领域的研究视野，为实验室开展作物抗性调控、微生物—作物互作等相关交叉研究提供了新思路与新方法，对推动团队科研创新与学术发展具有积极促进作用。

The content of this online academic exchange is cutting-edge and the results are solid, which effectively broadens the research field of the research group in the field of crop stress molecular biology. It provides new ideas and new methods for the laboratory to carry out cross-cutting research such as crop resistance regulation and microbial-crop interaction, and has a positive role in promoting team scientific research innovation and academic development.
